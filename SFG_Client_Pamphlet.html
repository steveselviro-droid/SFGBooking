How payment works

💳 Pay by bank transfer or e-wallet
  1. Message us to book your seat. We confirm the price.
  2. We send you our bank or e-wallet details in the same message.
  3. You make the payment and send us the proof (a screenshot or the transaction reference).
  4. Your bank reference becomes your booking reference — we send it back with your driver's details.
  5. Your seat is locked. You just show up at your time.
💵 Paying cash to the driver?

Cash is accepted, but it's slower. The driver has to collect and confirm before your seat is locked. Electronic payment confirms your seat instantly.

⏰ BOOKING CUTOFF

✅ Bookings close 1 hour before departure.

✅ Once closed, you cannot cancel.

Why pay electronically?

  • Instant confirmation — no waiting for the driver to collect.
  • Traceable — your bank reference is your booking reference.
  • Safer — no cash to carry, especially at night.
  • Faster refunds — straight back to your account.
  • Priority — paid clients get the seat first when a trip is nearly full.

Frequently asked questions

What if the trip doesn't fill?

You get a full refund, and we offer you the next departure at no extra cost. We only cancel when there aren't enough paid passengers — never to save us money.

What if I miss my trip?

Confirmed departures leave on time. If you miss it, the fare isn't refunded — but first-time misses get a goodwill credit toward your next trip. Message us and we'll help.

Can I book for someone else?

Yes. Give us their name and phone number, and we'll send the driver's details to both of you.

Can I send a parcel?

Yes — on any confirmed trip. Pricing depends on size and destination; ask us for a quote when you book.

How early should I arrive?

10 minutes before departure at the designated meeting point. That's it — no need to come earlier to hold a spot.

What if I need to change my booking?

Message us as early as possible. Changes are easier the further ahead you tell us.

📋 The simple rules
Book at least 1 hour before departure · arrive 10 minutes early at the designated pickup point · show your booking message when you board · one seat per paying passenger · electronic payment is faster and safer than cash.
🤝 Our promise: if we cancel your trip, you get every cent back. If we confirm it, the car leaves at the published time — not early, not late.
