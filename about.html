About SFG Booking

SFG (Safe, Fast & Guaranteed), also written SF&G, is a WhatsApp-based booked-seat transport service in Windhoek, Namibia. You book and pay for a seat ahead, pick a departure time, and travel with a driver whose name, plate and car you know before you go.

Our missionGive Namibian travellers their time back: a booked seat, a fixed departure time and no hours of waiting for a car to fill.
Fixed departure timeThe car leaves at the time you booked. Never early, never late.
Book and pay aheadYour seat is locked once your payment is verified. Bookings close 1 hour before departure.
Know your driverDriver’s name, plate, car model and colour are sent before you travel.
Parcels, with or without a seatSend a parcel on any trip. Choose Parcel on the booking form and we quote a price by size, weight and destination.

Why SFG exists

Travel between Namibian towns often starts with hours of waiting for a car to fill. We think the wait should never be longer than the time it takes to book. With SFG you book and pay for a seat ahead, pick a departure time, and the car leaves at that time.

What every traveller should expect

These are the basics we think every traveller is owed: a departure time, a driver you know, a reply when you ask, a reminder before you go and a seat that is already yours.

Looked after from booking to boarding

From your first WhatsApp message to the moment you board: a reply on time, a confirmation with your driver’s name, plate and car, a reminder an hour before, and help if your plans change.

Summer and holiday travel

Hot days and busy roads make waiting at the rank harder. Book ahead and you know your time, your seat and your driver.

Night travel and how to book

Late trips run at 20:00 and 21:00. You pay ahead and get a confirmed, traceable ride with a driver you know.
